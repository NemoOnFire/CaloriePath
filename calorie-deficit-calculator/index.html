Understanding a Calorie Deficit

A calorie deficit occurs when you consume fewer calories than your body uses for energy. Over time, maintaining a calorie deficit can lead to weight loss.

How does a calorie deficit work?

Your maintenance calories represent an estimate of the calories your body uses each day. This calculator subtracts your selected deficit from that estimate to create a daily calorie target.

How many calories should I cut to lose weight?

A commonly used starting point is a deficit of about 500 calories per day, which is roughly associated with 1 pound of weight loss per week. Individual results vary.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 500 calorie deficit?

A 500 calorie deficit means eating approximately 500 fewer calories per day than your estimated maintenance level.

How many calories should I eat to lose 1 pound a week?

A commonly used estimate is about 500 calories below your maintenance calories each day. Your actual calorie needs and results may differ.

Can I lose weight without counting calories?

Yes. Counting calories is one approach, but portion control, food choices, activity, and consistent eating habits can also help create a calorie deficit.

How accurate is a calorie deficit calculator?

A calorie deficit calculator provides an estimate based on the information you enter. Your actual calorie needs can vary, so your progress over time is useful for evaluating the estimate.
