What is a calorie deficit?

A calorie deficit occurs when you consume fewer calories than your body uses. Over time, this can lead to weight loss.

How does this calculator work?

The calculator subtracts your selected calorie deficit from your estimated maintenance calories. A deficit of about 500 calories per day corresponds to roughly 1 pound of weight loss per week as a general estimate.

Remember

Calorie needs vary from person to person. Use the result as a starting estimate and adjust based on your progress over time.
