What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It is an estimate of how many calories you burn in a typical day, including your resting energy needs and physical activity.

How is TDEE calculated?

This calculator estimates BMR using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and then multiplies BMR by an activity factor to estimate TDEE.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

A common starting point is a moderate calorie deficit from estimated maintenance calories. Your actual progress over several weeks is more useful than treating any calculator estimate as an exact number.

Why add a protein target?

Protein can help support muscle maintenance and satiety during a calorie deficit. This calculator provides a simple estimate that can be adjusted based on your goals and circumstances.
