What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It is an estimate of how many calories you burn in a typical day, including your resting energy needs and physical activity.

How is TDEE calculated?

This calculator estimates BMR using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and then multiplies BMR by an activity factor to estimate TDEE.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

A common starting point is a moderate calorie deficit from estimated maintenance calories. Your actual progress over several weeks is more useful than treating any calculator estimate as an exact number.

Why add a protein target?

Protein can help support muscle maintenance and satiety during a calorie deficit. This calculator provides a simple estimate that can be adjusted based on your goals and circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good calorie deficit for weight loss?

A moderate calorie deficit is a common starting point for weight loss. Your ideal calorie intake depends on your maintenance calories, activity level, and individual goals.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is the estimated number of calories your body needs at rest. TDEE includes your BMR plus the calories you burn through daily activity and exercise.

How accurate is a TDEE calculator?

A TDEE calculator provides an estimate rather than an exact number. Tracking your weight and calorie intake over several weeks can help you better understand your actual maintenance needs.

How much protein should I eat while losing weight?

A higher-protein diet can help support muscle maintenance and may help you feel fuller while eating fewer calories. Your ideal protein intake depends on your body size, activity level, and goals.
