What are macros?

Macronutrients, commonly called macros, are protein, carbohydrates, and dietary fat. They provide the energy and nutrients your body needs.

How are macros calculated?

Protein and carbohydrates provide about 4 calories per gram, while fat provides about 9 calories per gram. This calculator converts your selected calorie percentages into estimated grams of each macronutrient.

Why track macros?

Tracking macros can help you organize your nutrition around goals such as weight loss, muscle gain, or maintaining your current weight.
