Your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is an estimate of how many calories you burn each day based on your basal metabolic rate and activity level.

What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It estimates the total number of calories your body uses each day, including normal body functions, movement, and exercise.

What is BMR?

BMR, or Basal Metabolic Rate, is an estimate of the calories your body needs at rest to maintain basic functions such as breathing and circulation.

How accurate is a TDEE calculator?

A TDEE calculator provides an estimate rather than an exact measurement. Your actual calorie needs can vary based on activity, body composition, and other factors.
